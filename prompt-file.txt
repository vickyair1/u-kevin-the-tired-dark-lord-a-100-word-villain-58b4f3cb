Write a ~100-word monologue for the make.fun bounty
"The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue"
(u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

The speaker is a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The
tiredness must live in the voice, the rhythm and the small deflections —
stutters, losing the thread, trailing off, deflecting into small talk —
never in a plain statement that he is weary (never use "tired", "weary",
"exhausted" or similar). He must still be a villain: a specific one with
a history and a scheme, with concrete fantasy detail, not stock scenery.
It must read as speakable dialogue a performer could deliver in one
breath, with a turn or a sting in the last line, and spend every word —
cut anything that only repeats an earlier line.

Deliverable: main.py that prints the monologue deterministically
(byte-identical every run) and writes it to /output/entry.txt, plus
entry.txt with the same text. Exactly ~100 words, 1–600 characters.
